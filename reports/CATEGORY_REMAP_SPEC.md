## PART 10: THE AUDIT ITSELF IS A FINDING

This remapping was produced by a manual audit of all 5,680 products against their
raw product group labels, carried out after the first full analysis was complete.
It should be reported in the dissertation, not hidden.

Three things it established:

1. The store's raw labels include two catch-all groups. `ESSENTIAL FOOD ITEM`
   held 412 products, of which roughly 276 were not staples. `HOUSEHOLD ITEM`
   held 530, of which roughly 250 were cleaning products, air fresheners or pest
   control. Staff used these labels for anything without an obvious home.

2. Automated quality checks cannot detect this. The 32 checks verify completeness,
   referential integrity and cross-schema consistency. None of them can tell
   whether a product is in the right category, because that requires reading the
   product name and knowing what it is.

3. Two structural gaps in the store's category scheme were identified but not
   filled: there is no category for nuts and dry fruits, and none for sauces and
   condiments. Both would improve the analysis. Neither was added because the
   category count of 25 is load-bearing for the combinatorial framing in section
   05 and the feature ceiling in section 23.5. Both are recorded in future work.
